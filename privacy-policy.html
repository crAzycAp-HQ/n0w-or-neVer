Privacy Policy

This privacy policy is based on the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which applies to the site operator in the EU/Germany.

1. Controller

The party responsible for data processing on this website is:

2. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This website is hosted via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (a Microsoft Corporation company), based in the USA. When you visit the website, GitHub automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, date and time of access, browser type and the requested document. This processing is technically necessary to provide the website. More information is available in GitHub's privacy statement.

3. Embedded Twitch content

This website embeds the Twitch livestream and live chat. When the homepage or live section loads, a connection is automatically made to Twitch servers. Twitch may therefore learn that this website was accessed. Cookies may be set and data such as your IP address may be transferred to Twitch/Amazon, including to the USA. The embedding is based on our legitimate interest in presenting our stream. More information is available in Twitch's privacy policy.

4. Fonts

The fonts used on this website are loaded locally. No connection to external font-provider servers such as Google Fonts is made through the website.

5. Links to Discord

This website contains a link to our Discord server. The link only leads to Discord after an active click. Simply visiting this website does not transfer data to Discord through that link. Once you follow the link, Discord's privacy policy applies.

6. Your rights as a data subject

You have the right to:

  • Access the data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Erasure of your data (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority

To exercise these rights, contact us using the email address above.

We reserve the right to update this privacy policy when the functionality of this website changes, for example through a future merch shop, sponsors or analytics tools. The current version published on this page applies.
